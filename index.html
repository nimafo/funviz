Chat Heatmap

Turn a Telegram or WhatsApp chat into a GitHub-style heatmap of messages per day. Your file never leaves your browser.

1. Export your chat

  1. Install Telegram Desktop (the phone apps can't export).
  2. Open the chat you want and click ⋮ in the top-right corner → Export chat history.
  3. Untick photos, videos, files, etc. Only the text is needed, so the export is much faster.
  4. Set Format to Machine-readable JSON.
  5. Make sure the date range is From: the oldest message, then click Export.
  6. Find result.json in Downloads/Telegram Desktop/ChatExport_….

A full-account export (Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data, JSON) works too, even if it's many GB. You'll get a list of chats to choose from.

  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. iPhone: tap the contact or group name at the top → scroll down → Export Chat.
    Android: tap ⋮ → More → Export chat.
  3. Choose Without media.
  4. Save the file (e.g. to Files or Drive, or send it to yourself). iPhone makes a .zip and Android a .txt. Both work, no need to unzip.
  5. Open this page and pick the file. It works on your phone too.

Group chats work too. Only messages are counted. System notices like "X joined" are skipped.

2. Drop your export here

Drag & drop result.json, or a WhatsApp .txt / .zip or click to choose a file · processed locally, nothing is uploaded
